Our story

We created SEASONS to make fragrance discovery feel personal.

SEASONS is a student-led fragrance website designed around the idea that every scent carries a mood, a memory, and a moment. Our team combined research, design, development, and storytelling to create a welcoming digital experience for fragrance lovers.
